Four-Bug Hypothesis and Evidence

Bug Hypothesis Evidence / Test Result
1. Escaping badge The badge is positioned to the wrong containing block, causing it to escape the card. I inspected the card and badge in DevTools and tested the position relative on the card. The badge moved into and anchored to the card. Confirmed. Adding the positioning context to the card gave it the right the badge placement.
2. Trapped callout A stacking context on the lower container keeps the callout from appearing above the neighboring panel. I inspected the callout and its parent in DevTools and disabled the property creating the unwanted stacking context. After doing that, the callout was able to appear above the neighboring panel. Confirmed. Removing the unnecessary stacking context corrected the layering.
3. Blocked link The decorative overlay is above the link and intercepting pointer events. I tested pointer events: none on the decorative overlay in DevTools. I clicked the link and confirmed that it went to the right destination. Confirmed. The decorative overlay stays visible without blocking the link.
4. Obscuring sticky heading The sticky heading covers the focus target when the page link moves focus to it. I clicked the focus link, observed the target being obscured, disabled position: sticky in DevTools, and repeated the test. The target became fully visible. Confirmed. Removing the sticky positioning keepsthe heading from blocking the focus target.
